# Access, permissions and limitations

- The full 600-review WISEST dataset is not included here.
- Verbatim quotations and underlying review text may be subject to copyright, licence or contractual restrictions.
- Check approval from contributors, data custodians and any relevant ethics or data-sharing arrangements before releasing real annotations.
- The example record is fictional; no counts, assessments or findings may be inferred from it.
- The dataset addresses methodological appraisal of reviews, not direct assessment of frontier model dangerous capabilities.
- Expert labels should not be assumed infallible; uncertainty, adjudication and any rater agreement should be documented in the eventual methods release.
